## Root cause

The Candidate header rendered assisted profile, CV, and activation links from the mere presence of a Candidate ID. The actual assisted workspace independently called the canonical managed-Candidate status endpoint and correctly treated a `404` as a Candidate-managed account. That split produced contradictory truth: enabled hash links pointed to sections that were never rendered, while the workspace correctly said assisted mutation was unavailable.

Verification operation cards also rendered independently of their backend permissions. The dialog checked permissions only after a card had already been activated, creating a permission-mismatch affordance. Account and Trust & Safety controls were not fake: API42 contains their Admin-authorized endpoints and the frontend already consumed authoritative backend capability booleans. The account confirmation path nevertheless lacked an immediate single-flight lock around rapid duplicate confirmation.

## Fix summary

- Added a centralized Candidate-detail capability selector based on the canonical managed-Candidate read, canonical authenticated permissions, backend-projected account capabilities, and canonical active-session count.
- Replaced unconditional assisted hash links with semantic disabled buttons while managed eligibility is checking, denied, Candidate-managed, or unavailable.
- Added visible per-control explanations; disabled controls have no `href`, no active handler, no pointer affordance, and cannot be triggered by click, Enter, or Space.
- Kept eligible Admin-managed links pointed at the existing real profile, CV, and activation sections and contracts.
- Made Court, in-person, and internal-report launch cards and dialog tabs enforce their exact backend permission independently.
- Added immediate single-flight protection to account/security confirmation submission, in addition to the pending disabled state.
- Kept account/risk controls that API42 proves are real. No button was removed solely to hide a backend-supported action.
- Unsupported endpoints discovered: **None among the audited controls**.
- Buttons removed: **None**. Permission- or lifecycle-ineligible account/risk controls remain intentionally hidden; assisted and verification controls remain visible-disabled where the explanation is useful.

## Eligibility, permission, refresh, and error truth

- Candidate-managed accounts remain fully readable. Canonical managed-status `404` disables profile, CV, and activation without emitting a mutation request.
- Admin-managed `admin_created_unclaimed`, `activation_pending`, and `claimed` states continue through the existing status/read contract. Activation actions are lifecycle-specific and a claimed account renders a non-action status.
- A managed-status error fails closed. It cannot expose assisted operations from stale display labels or from the Candidate ID alone.
- Account/security actions remain driven by backend-projected capabilities, not frontend role guesses.
- Trust & Safety creation remains driven by canonical `risk.create` and the real API42 investigation endpoint.
- Mutation success invalidates and refetches canonical query state. No local optimistic account, verification, or investigation truth was introduced.
- API errors continue through sanitized `ApiError` handling, including 401, 403, 404, 409, 422, and 5xx responses.
